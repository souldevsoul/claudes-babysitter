## Part 1: NEW-CHECK specs (grouped)

Ids reuse an existing id when the spec extends that check. "Hard"/"soft" follow the tool's conventions. Each fixture: **bad**
(reproduces the card, must fire), **good** (must pass), **look-alike** (must stay quiet).

### G1 · CT1 extend: en "not switched on yet" / "once … is live" (static + rendered)
Cards: 4670bf, yvewhx, dqdasz, evwgc7, chqsch, dgqpux, rsp5rt.
Why 5.4.1 misses: `UNFINISHED` has `switched off`, `not (yet )?live( yet)?`, `live yet` but nothing for `not switched on yet` or
`once … is live` (the az strings "hələ aktiv deyil" were caught, the en ones never).
Rule: add to `UNFINISHED` (`lib/content-rules.js`, so static, rendered and acceptance B1 all get it):
`/\bnot (yet )?(switched|turned) on( yet)?\b/i`, `/\b(once|until|when) [\w\s'-]{1,40}? (is|are|goes|go) live\b/i`,
`/\bis not (yet )?(enabled|active)( yet)?\b/i` (a feature, not an account state: skip when it follows "your account"/"2FA"/"two-factor").
- bad: `<p>Card payment is not switched on yet, so credits are added by our team.</p>`; `<li>Card details: once card payment is live, the details pass to our processor.</li>`
- good: `<p>Card payments are processed by our card processor; credits are added at once.</p>`
- look-alikes: `<p>Notifications are switched on by default.</p>`; `<p>Two-factor sign-in is not enabled yet on your account. Turn it on in Settings.</p>` (account state); `<p>Our support line is live from 9:00 to 18:00.</p>`

### G2 · 2.5 extend: a price range split at its dash, in every currency of the switcher (rendered)
Cards: 34ebhu, rqgkrl, 9rkp6z, ct4lge, 69q0rj (the value-wrap half).
Why 5.4.1 misses: `layout.js textBreaks` treats a break after `–` followed by a non-digit as "a real break opportunity"
(`/[-‐‑–—/_.,:;)\]]/.test(before) && !/\d/.test(t[cut])` → `continue`), so "€44.54–|€98" passes; and `owner()` skips text inside
`p, li, td` (where these ranges live); and textBreaks runs only in the current currency (`moneyFormat` switches currency, 2.5 does not).
Rule: for every visible text run (any owner, also `p/li/td/dd`), find ranges `AMOUNT\s?[–—-]\s?AMOUNT` (amounts per `MONEY_SRC`, or
bare numbers followed by a unit: `cr`, `credits`, `/hr`, `KR`, `кредит`). If the range's Range rects span 2+ line tops → hard
"range split across lines: '€44.54–|€98' at 360px — keep the range together (white-space:nowrap on the range, or U+2060/U+00A0 around
the dash)". Run textBreaks again after `moneyFormat` switches each currency (same loop, same restore), and in run.spec i18n.
- bad: `<div style="width:150px;font:14px sans-serif"><p>500–1,100 · €44.54–€98</p></div>` at 360 (wraps after the dash)
- good: `<p>500–1,100 · <span style="white-space:nowrap">€44.54–€98</span></p>`
- look-alikes: `<p>Open Monday–Friday, 9:00–18:00 in a long sentence that wraps somewhere else…</p>` (not amounts; the break is not inside the range); a range on two lines by layout `<div><span style="display:block">$60–</span><span style="display:block">$95</span></div>` is still bad, but `<dl><dt>From</dt><dd>$60</dd><dt>To</dt><dd>$95</dd></dl>` stays quiet.

### G3 · 2.12 extend: a key–value row that breaks its list's pattern (rendered)
Cards: 69q0rj, x2okne, k25bx0, 25x6e8 (the amounts half).
Why 5.4.1 misses: `checks.js repeatedRowColumns` needs rows of one component with the same child count and reports only columns 2+
whose left, right and centre all spread > 3px. The odd row ("RATE BAND", "HOURLY RATE") has a different structure (no leader, a
two-part value), so it is filtered out of the comparison instead of reported.
Rule: in a stack of ≥ 3 rows that share one parent and are flex/grid rows with a label child first (≥ 2 of them have the same class
signature), take the majority pattern: value right edge (±3px), a leader element between label and value (present/absent), label
line count. A row that differs in value right edge by > 6px, lacks the leader the others have, or whose label wraps while the others'
do not → hard "row 'Rate band' breaks the pattern of its 3 siblings (value right edge 288 vs 344; no leader)". Rows whose value
column's vertical alignment differs (baseline of first line vs centred) → soft.
- bad: three rows `<div class=row><span>Specialists</span><i class=dots></i><b>15</b></div>` + `<div class=row-alt><span>Rate band (cr / hr)</span><b>125–180 · €11.10–€16</b></div>` at 360px, value wraps and sits left
- good: four identical `.row` with leader and right-aligned value
- look-alikes: a definition list whose last row is a full-width note (`<p class=note>` spanning both columns, no value); a table (2.4 owns it).

### G4 · 2.17 new: text squeezed into a narrow column; 2.11 extend: glyphs of two texts overlap (rendered)
Cards: akf9f4, 2qp6hw, 3ypscy, 25x6e8.
Why 5.4.1 misses: nothing measures words per line. `overlappingText` (2.11) samples only the element's centre with
`elementFromPoint`, only inside the viewport, and needs the covering element to have a background, so a transparent caption over a
heading passes.
Rule 2.17: for each text block with ≥ 10 words: lines = distinct line tops of its Range rects; words/line < 2.2 AND (its own width
< 35% of its nearest painted/flex ancestor's content width OR that ancestor has ≥ 40% of its content width empty beside the block) →
hard "text squeezed to 1–2 words per line (25 lines, 64px wide in a 328px card) — let it take the card's width (min-width:0 /
flex-basis / grid column)". Every viewport, every language (run.spec i18n), every state.
Rule 2.11 overlap: for every pair of text runs in different leaf elements whose Range rects intersect by ≥ 2px in both axes (whole
page, not only the viewport; skip `aria-hidden`, alpha ≤ 0.25, `position:fixed/sticky` layers) → hard "'BEFORE THE FIRST MESSAGE'
overlaps 'Cancelled work'".
- bad: `<div style="display:flex;width:328px"><h3 style="white-space:nowrap">Cancelled work</h3><p style="width:min-content">Cancel before the specialist writes on the thread and the credits return to your wallet in full.</p></div>`; overlap: `<div style="position:relative"><h3>Cancelled work</h3><span style="position:absolute;top:0;left:60px">BEFORE THE FIRST MESSAGE</span></div>`
- good: `<div style="display:flex;flex-direction:column;width:328px"><h3>Cancelled work</h3><p>Cancel before…</p></div>`
- look-alikes: a narrow sidebar list of single words (`<li>Audio</li>`… < 10 words each); a poem/ list in `white-space:pre-line`; a decorative watermark word behind text with alpha 0.1.

### G5 · 2.18 new: two-column blocks share one alignment rule (rendered, per page + site-wide, ≥ 1024px)
Cards: 1wq9va, 76glyk, uiv7nb, 2ghvum, tp3fvq, khokf3.
Why 5.4.1 misses: `layout.js alignment` reports side-by-side first lines only when they have the **same font size** and differ by
**2–12px** (soft). These offsets are 20–75px, between a heading and a table of different sizes.
Rule: a block = a visible element with exactly two in-flow children side by side (vertical ranges overlap), each ≥ 25% of its
width, both holding text. Left anchors = tops of the first ink of each direct text child of the left column (eyebrow, heading,
paragraphs). R = top of the right column's first ink. The block's rule = the index of the anchor with |R − anchor| ≤ 4px, else none.
(a) hard: rule none and the nearest anchor is > 12px away ("the right column starts 38px above the paragraph and 20px below the
heading: it lines up with nothing"); (b) hard, site-wide: blocks on the site use more than one rule (list each block and its rule);
(c) hard: in one block, a left and a right text block of the same font size whose tops differ 4–32px (tp3fvq: 484 vs 463); (d) soft:
bottoms differ > 48px and the shorter column has an empty band. Right columns centred on purpose (`align-self/align-items:center`)
and image-only columns are skipped.
- bad: `<section style="display:grid;grid-template-columns:1fr 1fr;width:1200px"><div><p style="font-size:12px">INDEX</p><h1 style="margin-top:24px">Every specialist</h1><p>Lead</p></div><div style="padding-top:20px"><table><tr><td>Specialists</td><td>15</td></tr></table></div></section>`
- good: same with `padding-top:0` (right column on the eyebrow line), and every other two-column block of the site also on its first line
- look-alikes: a hero whose right column is an illustration centred with `align-self:center`; a single two-column block on the whole site (b needs ≥ 2 blocks); two columns whose texts have different sizes and differ by 40px but the right top matches the left heading top (rule = heading).

### G6 · 2.19 new: even rhythm in a list or a row of options (rendered, every state)
Cards: xfm2up, iiy7tk.
Why 5.4.1 misses: `emptySpace` (2.7) looks at gaps between page sections (> 2.5× median and > 160px); nothing compares gaps between
items of one list or one row.
Rule: ≥ 3 visible siblings of one class signature, stacked or in a row. Gaps g_i between consecutive boxes. Hard when one gap
> 1.6 × median gap and ≥ 8px larger (xfm2up: one empty line in the Legal column), or, in a row, when gaps spread > 6px or the row's
inset from its container is asymmetric by > 12px (iiy7tk: 43 vs 16px). A gap that holds a separator (`hr`, a border, a heading) or
a group boundary (`ul` → `ul`) is not a gap.
- bad: `<ul style="display:flex;flex-direction:column;gap:8px"><li><a>Terms</a></li><li><a>Refund</a></li><li><a>Cancellation</a></li><li style="margin-top:24px"><a>AML / KYC</a></li><li><a>Manage cookies</a></li></ul>`
- good: same without the margin
- look-alikes: two `ul`s in one column with a 24px gap between them; a list with an `<li role=separator>`; a centred row of 3 cards whose insets are equal.

### G8 · 3.9 new: a selection's result opens on screen (rendered, states, every viewport edge)
Cards: gn14vd, vu8j0h, 2t4uiv, 1fxg7h, r2jy12 (r2jy12 also needs G9).
